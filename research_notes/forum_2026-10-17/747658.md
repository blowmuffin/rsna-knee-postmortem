      TASK: For each of the 12 findings below, judge from the IMAGES whether the finding meets the POSITIVE criterion. Emulate expert annotators: borderline or uncertain findings are NEGATIVE (specificity over sensitivity).
      
      POSITIVE CRITERIA (official):
      
      ACL: high-grade partial (>50% fibers) or full-thickness tear: discontinuity, abnormal course/laxity on sagittal. Negative: mild signal change / mucoid thickening with fibers intact.
      MCL: high-grade ACUTE tear with disrupted fibers + periligamentous edema (coronal FS). Negative: low-grade sprain / thickening only.
      Medial/Lateral Meniscus: abnormal signal reaching an articular surface on >=2 slices, OR morphologic tear (truncated, displaced, extruded, root tear). Negative: intrasubstance signal not reaching surface.
      Medial/Lateral/PF OA: >=~1 cm of HIGH-GRADE cartilage loss (>50% thickness) in that compartment. Negative: small/low-grade chondromalacia.
      Effusion: MODERATE or LARGE joint-fluid distension (FS bright, esp. suprapatellar pouch on sagittal). Negative: trace/physiologic.
      Synovitis: synovial thickening/hypertrophy beyond mere fluid, villonodular change, Hoffa fat-pad edema/impingement. Negative: effusion alone.
      Baker's: MODERATE or LARGE popliteal fluid collection between gastrocnemius and semimembranosus (sagittal FS, medial-posterior). Negative: tiny recess fluid.
      Contusion: geographic marrow edema (dark on T1/non-FS, bright on FS) WITHOUT a fracture line — impact bruise pattern. Negative: edema confined to subchondral degenerative change or containing a clear fracture line.
      Fracture: cortical break or discrete fracture line (dark line through marrow on non-FS, or depressed articular surface), incl. insufficiency/impaction. Negative: chronic deformity/osteophytes.

    - [Kirderf | 2026-10-09T11:42 | v0] Yes working with image vs report is the objective, one of the experiments lately:
      
      - Read every report several independent times.
      
      - Fuse the readers into one probability per cell.
      Combine the per-reader probabilities into a single fused probability per
      (study, finding) cell — use a weighted mean over the reader pool.
      Keep, per cell: the fused probability, the number of readers that
      answered (__nsrc), their disagreement (__spread = max−min), and a
      confidence (__conf).
      
      - Find the cells most likely to be wrong.
      
      - Re-read the actual report text per cell.
      
      - Apply verdicts onto the fused table.
      
      - Fill the silent cells with measured priors, not zero.

# Are RadImageNet pretrained weights allowed in a final submission?
750260 | Karan Bhatti (@sircausticmail) | 2026-10-17T13:42 | votes -20

About 60% of the public notebooks at 0.90+ load a RadImageNet ResNet-50, or heads trained on top of it (e.g. marwanmath/resnet-50-radimagenet-marwan, antoinegg1/rsna-knee-e9-radimagenet-heads-v15, prvsiyan/rsna-knee-v52-radimagenet-heads-20260812). Anyone can download the weights from Google Drive, linked from the RadImageNet GitHub . The images they were trained on are "available by request" only. The licence is unclear: the repository lists MIT, and 735121  says CC BY-NC-SA.

Questions:

- Is a public pretrained model allowed when its training images are shared only on request?

- If the weights are CC BY-NC-SA, is that fine under rule 2.5.a.4 (pretrained models with an incompatible licence keep their own)?

A yes or no is enough. Thank you.

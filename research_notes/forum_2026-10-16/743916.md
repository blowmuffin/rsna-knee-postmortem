    - [Quan Vu | 2026-09-29T11:53 | v3] I think there are a few reasons. First, frontier models have leveled out skill across the community, and the gaps on the leaderboard are now very small. Second, LLMs are extremely good at reading and absorbing information: any finding posted on the forum gets picked up by almost every team right away. When the margins are only a few thousandths, sharing during a competition basically means giving away your own edge. Players still have to balance self-interest against the community's interest, but the scale has clearly tipped. Third, technical discussion is shifting from the forum to direct conversations with AI agents. These days, the most common question on the forum is "what does the host want?", which is something an agent can't answer. So I think the trend will be to share findings after a competition ends, rather than while it's still running.

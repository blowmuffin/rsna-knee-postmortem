# Request for official clarification: Rule 2.6(a) and registration-gated public datasets (MRNet, OAI, etc.)
735497 | Demir Poyraz Elcin | 2026-08-16T09:52 | votes 8

Rule 2.6(a) requires external data to be "publicly available and equally accessible to use by all Participants at no cost." Several widely used public knee-MRI datasets (e.g., Stanford MRNet, OAI) are free of charge but require registration or a data-use agreement before download.

Could the hosts confirm whether such registration-gated datasets satisfy 2.6(a) for this competition — and if so, whether pre-training on them is permitted?

At least two earlier participant questions on this point appear to be unanswered. An official ruling would help everyone plan fairly. Thank you!

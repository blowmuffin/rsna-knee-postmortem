# 0.950 with a second architecture: Heliosli's S6 ConvNeXt retrained on our labels
749359 | Danial Zakaria | 2026-10-14T18:52 | votes 4

Following up on our 0.949 CoAtNet post : a second, unrelated single model now scores 0.950 public AUC. The notebook that produced the score is public, rsna-knee-s6fall-solo , and the weights are in nartaa/rsna-knee-s6fall-weights  (CC BY-NC 4.0, SHA-256 0c7297d5…371c12). One click to reproduce: open the notebook, Copy & Edit, Submit. From version 2 every input is public: our weights, Heliosli's anatomy checkpoint and data pipeline  and our 0.949 weights , the latter used only for a study the ConvNeXt cannot read. Each code file and checkpoint is checked against the sha256 of the scored version 1, and version 2's commit output is byte-identical to version 1's.

What it is

We took Heliosli's  S6 pipeline, his DICOM-to-rows path and his compact_train.py / compact_infer.py, and retrained it on our own label set. We did not use his S6 weights, which he has since retired. The model has these parts:

- Backbone: convnext_small.fb_in22k_ft_in1k, with per-finding spatial attention and window-attention MIL over the study.

- Views: the full field of view, plus 100 mm knee-centred local crops. The crops are placed with the SKM-TEA anatomy V-Net anchors from Heliosli's public checkpoint . Images are cached at 320 px and cropped to 288, with 3 slices per window and 3.5 mm physical spacing.

- Labels: our JEV-blended soft labels (the blend described in the 0.949 post), with a prior fill for unaddressed cells and a weight-normalised BCE.

- Training: 64 windows per study, 16 epochs, AdamW (lr 1e-4 for the encoder and 1e-3 for the head, wd 0.05), batch 2, cosine schedule with 5 % warm-up, bf16, seed 42. It trains on all 4,263 report-labelled studies. The 58 gold studies were excluded.

- Inference: Heliosli's compact_infer.py, unchanged except for fp16 instead of bf16, because the T4 has no bf16. Any study the ConvNeXt fails on falls back to our 0.949 CoAtNet.

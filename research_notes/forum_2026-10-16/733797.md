# Eligibility under 18
733797 | ValkBlox | 2026-08-08T09:06 | votes 0

Are participants under 18 eligible, with parental/guardian consent?

§3.1.a permits participants below the age of majority "unless otherwise agreed to by Competition Sponsor and appropriate parental/guardian consents have been obtained." Kaggle maintains a formal consent process for minors, but notes it applies only to competitions that allow minors, and the competition-specific rules don't address it either way.

I mentor secondary-school students (ages 15–16, India) and would like to include them on a team. Could the host confirm whether minors are eligible for this competition with completed Kaggle consent forms, and whether any additional sponsor approval is needed?

Related: if minors are not eligible, I take it §2.4.b and §3.6.a mean I cannot show competition data or competition code to students who are not registered participants, even in a teaching context. A confirmation either way would be useful to others running student groups.
